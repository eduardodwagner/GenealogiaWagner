History



Between 1763 and 1767 my ancestors (and thousands of people too) left behind German land for several reasons :

1) The constant wars in Europe didn´t give stability to german civility.

2) From Russia germans were encouraged to settle by the banks of the Volga River, granting to each family an extensive parcel of land for their sowings, allowing them to live in villages on Russian land preserving their religion, education, languaje, etc.

3) All sons of german inmigrants were free of military service, that in those days lasted from 5 to 7 years. So around 1767 they established on the right bank of the Volga River, at Samara, Russia. There they remained almost 110 years because in 1878 when the "law of the one hundred years" falls, all the promises are no longer fulfilled. They depart to América on the steamship "Valparaiso II" landing in Buenos Aires (February 1878) and finally they selected the Province of Buenos Aires, Argentina, founding the Colonia Nievas.
